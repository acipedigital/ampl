\# AMPL for Enterprise \& Institutional Users



AMPL (Acipe Minimal Permissive License v1.0) is a permissive, non-copyleft license

designed to minimize legal and operational friction while preserving recognition

of original authorship upon redistribution.



AMPL is suitable for commercial, OEM, institutional, academic, and archival use.



---



\## Key Properties Relevant to Enterprise Use



AMPL includes:



\- No source disclosure requirements

\- No license inheritance or share-alike provisions

\- Closed-source redistribution is explicitly permitted

\- Attribution is required only upon redistribution and may be satisfied through

&nbsp; documentation or credits

\- No termination clauses

\- No patent clauses

\- No network-use provisions



---



\## Typical Enterprise Use Cases



AMPL is suitable for:



\- internal tools and utilities

\- embedded software and firmware

\- commercial desktop or server applications

\- long-lived systems requiring future maintenance

\- academic or institutional software distribution

\- OEM and white-label redistribution



AMPL is especially well-suited where:



\- legal clarity matters

\- compliance overhead must be minimal

\- long-term survivability is a concern



---



\## Summary for Legal Review



AMPL provides permissive rights comparable to MIT/BSD licenses, with explicit

clarification around attribution, voluntary contribution, and preservation.



AMPL imposes no copyleft obligations and no disclosure requirements.
