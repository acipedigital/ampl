\# Governance \& Stewardship (AMPL-1.0)



This document describes how the Acipe Minimal Permissive License v1.0 (AMPL-1.0)

is maintained and stewarded.



AMPL is designed to be stable, permissive, and low-friction. Its governance is

intentionally simple.



---



\## 1. Purpose



AMPL exists to provide a permissive license that:



\- grants broad rights of use, modification, and redistribution (including commercial and closed-source),

\- preserves recognition of original authorship upon redistribution,

\- welcomes voluntary contribution and collaboration without requiring it,

\- and explicitly supports preservation and long-term continuity.

\## 3. Canonical Text and Stability



\### 3.1 Canonical Text



The canonical license text for AMPL-1.0 is the file:



\- `LICENSE.txt`



Any copies elsewhere (websites, mirrors, reposts) should be treated as informational

unless they match the canonical text exactly.



\### 3.2 Stability Promise



AMPL-1.0 is intended to be stable and permanent.



\- The text of AMPL-1.0 will not be changed.

\- Clarifications, examples, and documentation may be updated, but these materials

&nbsp; are non-binding and do not alter the license text.



---



\## 4. Versioning Policy



If a future version is created:



\- it will be published under a new version number (e.g., AMPL-1.1, AMPL-2.0),

\- it will not apply retroactively to software released under AMPL-1.0,

\- projects may continue using AMPL-1.0 indefinitely.



No project is expected or required to upgrade.



---



\## 5. Changes to Documentation (Non-Binding)



The following materials may be revised over time:



\- `docs/FAQ.md`

\- `docs/EXAMPLES.md`

\- `docs/COMPARISON.md`

\- `docs/REVIEWER-NOTES.md`

\- `docs/BACKGROUND.md`

\- `docs/ECOSYSTEM.md`

\- `docs/REFERENCES.md`



Documentation updates are intended to improve clarity, onboarding, and real-world

usability. They do not modify the meaning of AMPL-1.0 and must not introduce new

requirements that conflict with the license text.



---



\## 6. Decision-Making Process



AMPL governance is conservative by design.



\### 6.1 Scope of Decisions



Stewardship decisions include:



\- maintaining a canonical copy of AMPL-1.0,

\- publishing documentation updates,

\- correcting typos or formatting issues in documentation,

\- responding to community questions and adoption feedback,

\- coordinating external submissions (e.g., SPDX / OSI) if pursued.



Stewardship decisions do not include imposing requirements on downstream users,

projects, or communities.



\### 6.2 How Decisions Are Made



\- The steward (Acipe Digital LLC) maintains the canonical repository.

\- The author may approve or reject documentation changes that create ambiguity

&nbsp; about AMPL-1.0’s meaning.

\- Major documentation changes should be proposed via issues or pull requests and

&nbsp; reviewed publicly.



---



\## 7. Community Participation



Feedback and discussion are welcome.



\- Issues and pull requests may be opened to propose improvements to documentation,

&nbsp; examples, and adoption guidance.

\- Changes are evaluated on clarity, correctness, and alignment with AMPL’s intent:

&nbsp; permissive rights, attribution upon redistribution, voluntary contribution, and

&nbsp; preservation support.



Participation is voluntary. There is no expectation of ongoing contribution.



---



\## 8. Trademarks and Naming



AMPL does not grant trademark rights.



If any project names, logos, or branding are used in examples or documentation,

they remain the property of their respective owners.



---



\## 9. Non-Endorsement



Mention of any products, projects, communities, or historical references is for

context only and does not imply endorsement.



Attribution required by AMPL recognizes origin only and must not be presented as

endorsement by the original author(s).
